  1. Open Album Artwork Finder
  2. Click on the correct album cover, then press Download 5000x5000px. (If this option is unavailable, you can select another resolution.)
  3. The album artwork will open in a new tab. Copy its URL and paste it below.
Info

You can use any valid image URL you like. You don't need to use Album Artwork Finder.

To find the CCLI ID:

  1. Open CCLI SongSelect and search for the song.
  2. Open the song's page, then copy the URL.
  3. The song's CCLI ID is the number near the end of the URL, after /Songs/ and before the song name. Paste it below.

To find the Music ID:

  1. Open Music and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then Copy Link .
  3. The song's Music ID is the number at the end of the URL you just copied. Paste it below and check you got the right song ID using the preview on the right.

To find the Spotify ID:

  1. Open Spotify and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then hover over Share and click Copy link to Song.
  3. The song's Spotify ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between /track/ and ?si=. Paste it below and check you got the right song ID using the preview on the right.

To find the YouTube Music ID:

  1. Open YouTube and search for the song.
  2. Open the song's page and press Share, then Copy.
  3. The song's YouTube Music ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between youtu.be/ and ?si=. Paste it below and check you got the right song ID using the preview on the right.
