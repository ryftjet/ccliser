CCLIser

Music sharing made easy

Learn more Try it out

CCLIser makes it easy to share church songs with your friends, band buddies and congregation with one link that takes every user to their preferred streaming platform.

You select the song, the artist and the recording, and get one link you can send to everybody. Play the version you want to perform, not the version that comes up first in search results.

CCLIser is free forever. There's no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Because there is no greater cause than bringing people to Christ.
