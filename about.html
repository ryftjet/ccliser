How do I use CCLIser?

CCLIser is a tool for sharing songs from CCLI with others using one link that offers viewers the option to open the song in CCLI, Music, Spotify or YouTube Music. CCLIser URLs should use the following format:

https://ryftjet.github.io/ccliser/?name=Song%20Name&artist=Artist%20Name&ccli=123456789&am=123456798&sp=abc123&yt=abc123&aa=https://example.com/image.jpg

The simplest way to create a CCLIser URL is by using this form. If you'd like more technical info on the URL format, read on!

The URL must include query parameters for at least the song name and the CCLI ID. Otherwise, the link will not work. All other query parameters are optional. Every query parameter should be URL-encoded, such as by using name=John%20Smith instead of name=John Smith, etc.

Parameter Meaning
name The name of the song
artist The name of the artist/s
ccli The song's ID in CCLI
am The song's ID in Music
sp The song's ID in Spotify
yt The song's ID in YouTube / YouTube Music
aa The URL of the song's album artwork

There are numerous options for finding album artwork for a song, such as Album Artwork Finder.

To find a song's ID on a particular platform, follow the instructions below:

CCLI SongSelect

  1. Open CCLI SongSelect and search for the song.
  2. Open the song's page, then copy the URL.
  3. The song's CCLI ID is the number near the end of the URL, after /Songs/ and before the song name.

Music

Web
  1. Open Music and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then Copy Link .
  3. The song's Music ID is the number at the end of the URL you just copied.
Desktop
  1. Open the Music app and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then hover over Share and click Copy Link.
  3. The song's Music ID is the number at the end of the URL you just copied, after ?i=.
Mobile
  1. Open the Music app and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then Share, then Copy.
  3. The song's Music ID is the number at the end of the URL you just copied, between ?i= and ?l=.

Spotify

Web & Desktop
  1. Open Spotify and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then hover over Share and click Copy link to Song.
  3. The song's Spotify ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between /track/ and ?si=.
Mobile
  1. Open the Spotify app and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then Share, then Copy link.
  3. The song's Spotify ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between /track/ and ?si=.

YouTube Music

YouTube Music is just YouTube with a slightly different interface and only music videos, so you can use either platform to get the song's YouTube Music ID.

YouTube
  1. Open YouTube and search for the song.
  2. Open the song's page and press Share, then Copy.
  3. The song's YouTube Music ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between youtu.be/ and ?si=.
YouTube Music
  1. Open YouTube Music and search for the song.
  2. Next to the song, press , then Share, then Copy.
  3. The song's YouTube Music ID is the sequence near the middle of the URL you just copied, between /watch?v= and ?si=.
